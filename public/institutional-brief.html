Gapwise Open Source

Campus Intelligence & Schedule Movement Platform

First-Year Student, University of Toronto Mississauga
gapwise.ca · support@gapwise.ca · GitHub: GapwiseHQ
Executive Brief: Gapwise is a free, independent student utility that bridges the gap between official course schedules and physical campus navigation. It connects class times to campus geometry—computing true usable break times, verified walking paths, step-free navigation, and study destinations across 11 Canadian universities and 13 campus models with strict privacy-by-design.
Core Student Functionality
  • Timetable Integration: Parses exports (.ics, ACORN, Carleton Central, RAMSS) entirely locally in the browser with zero cloud schedule ingestion.
  • True Usable Gap Budgeting: Automatically subtracts walking travel time, room transitions, and meal periods from breaks to highlight genuine study windows.
  • Pedestrian Navigation & Routing: Turn-by-turn outdoor walking routes between verified campus building entrances, including step-free accessible paths.
  • Campus Places & Residences: Source-backed dining and study spots with transparent hours, plus housing wayfinding across 124 residences.
Institutional Coverage

Currently deployed with dedicated subdomains and validated campus models for 11 institutions:

U of T (UTM, UTSG, UTSC) Carleton University Toronto Metropolitan (TMU) York University McMaster University Queen's University Western University Wilfrid Laurier University of Guelph University of Ottawa Brock University
Privacy & Security Architecture
  • Zero-PII Guest Mode: Full timetable and routing capabilities work instantly without account creation or email address.
  • Client-Side Processing: Calendar files are parsed in the browser via WebAssembly/TypeScript; raw schedules never touch our servers.
  • Application-Layer Encryption: Optional cloud sync encrypts data in the browser (Web Crypto API) before ciphertext is saved.
  • Responsible Disclosure: Published Vulnerability Disclosure Policy and .well-known/security.txt; audited permission models.
Developer & Ecosystem Tools
  • Public Campus API: Versioned REST endpoints (/v1/buildings, /v1/routes) for open campus data.
  • Official SDKs: Published TypeScript/JavaScript (@gapwise/sdk) and Python (gapwise) client libraries.
  • Model Context Protocol (MCP): Remote AI connector (ai.gapwise.ca) for privacy-preserving assistant integrations.
  • Developer CLI: Open-source CLI tool (@gapwise/cli) for campus data validation and integration testing.
